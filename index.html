    DefCon Radar — the cheap points machines

    26/27 rules are unchanged from 25/26: a defender scores 2 pts for 10+ clearances+blocks+interceptions+tackles in a match; a midfielder needs 12+, with ball recoveries added. Goalkeepers score none. Hit% is the share of 25/26 starts where the player actually cleared his threshold — the number that matters, because a forget-about-him defender needs to hit it most weeks, not average well. xPts/start = 2 appearance + 2×Hit% + clean-sheet points from the Elo model over GW1–12, minus expected goals-conceded deductions. Attacking returns are excluded, so this is a floor, not a projection.
    Counter-signal worth knowing: 26/27 cut the BPS value of clearances/blocks/interceptions from 1 per 2 to 1 per 3, explicitly to reduce centre-backs winning bonus. The DefCon floor is intact; the bonus ceiling on these players is lower than last season. (premierleague.com, official)

    Audition Room — try a player, see the future

    Swap anyone into your XI and watch the projection move. Each player's expected points per gameweek are built from four things: appearance points weighted by how often he actually started last season, DefCon points from his 25/26 hit rate, clean-sheet and goals-conceded points from the Elo fixture model, and attacking returns from his 25/26 xG and xA per 90 scaled by how favourable each fixture is. This is a floor-and-trend tool, not a crystal ball — it cannot know about a new role, a new manager, or form. Use it to compare two options over a run of fixtures, which is what it's honestly good at.

    Elite Managers

    Why there are no live squads here yet. FPL re-issues every manager's team ID from scratch each season, handed out in the order squads are submitted — so every elite manager's ID published before 23 July 2026 now resolves to a different, random person. No 2026/27 IDs have been published by anyone yet; the community articles carrying them appear after GW1. Loading a stale ID would silently show you the wrong squad, which is worse than showing nothing.

    What unlocks and when. From roughly 23 August, the global “Overall” league (id 314) exposes the current world top managers with their live IDs, and Fantasy Football Scout republishes its elite roster. Claude will pull the top squads in then, verifying each ID against the manager's name before trusting it. Until that point, the two tables below are the real, verified history.

    The better substitute: aggregate elite ownership

    Tracking twenty individuals is noisier than tracking what the whole elite tier converges on. These sources publish ownership and effective ownership cut by rank band, free, and become live once the season starts. LiveFPL is the only one offering a genuine top-100 and top-1k cut.
    • LiveFPL — top 10k ownership — selectable sample: Elite (top 100), top 1k, through top 10k, plus custom queries like “what share of the top 1k own exactly these players”.
    • Fantasy Football Pundit — effective ownership — live EO and captaincy, sampled from the top 10k within ~30 minutes of the deadline, widening afterwards. No paywall.
    • LiveFPL — live top 10k — live average score and captaincy split during matches.

    Expert Board

    Ranked by verified playing record, not audience size — because the two correlate less than you'd expect. Records come from top10k.co.uk, which certifies creators' top-10k finishes, plus each expert's own stated finishes. Tier A means a genuinely elite record; Tier B means good but not elite; Tier C means famous for something other than a current FPL record. Where a squad or captain call is behind a paywall I've said so rather than guessing.

    Beating a shrewd manager — the boring truth

    • Shrewd managers rarely lose leagues on bad picks — they lose them on chip timing and one emotional -8. Deny him both edges: your chips are planned on the Chips tab, and every transfer sits 24h before it's confirmed.
    • Track his differentials, not his template. Matching his good template picks costs nothing; his differentials are where he either wins the league or hands it to you.
    • If you're behind after GW30, the maths says take on risk then — not in October.
